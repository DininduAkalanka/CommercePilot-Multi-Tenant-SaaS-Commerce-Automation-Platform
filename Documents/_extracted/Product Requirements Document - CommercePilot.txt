1. Executive Summary
CommercePilot is an AI-powered commerce automation platform designed to integrate with existing e-
commerce systems such as WooCommerce, Shopify (future), and custom online stores.
Instead of replacing existing platforms, CommercePilot acts as an intelligent automation layer that
understands customer conversations, automates repetitive business processes, and assists business
owners with AI-driven decision support while maintaining human approval over critical operations.
The primary objective of the MVP is to automate WhatsApp-based order processing and synchronize
approved orders with an existing WooCommerce store.
2. Problem Statement
Many SMEs receive customer orders through WhatsApp instead of directly through their websites.
Business owners currently perform the following tasks manually:
Reading customer messages
Understanding customer intent

Checking inventory

✓ Audit log recorded
10. Business Rules
AI cannot approve orders automatically unless explicitly enabled.
Products must exist in the tenant catalogue.
Every order belongs to one tenant.
Every action must be logged.
Business owners always have the final decision.
Failed integrations must be retried.
Sensitive customer data must be protected.
