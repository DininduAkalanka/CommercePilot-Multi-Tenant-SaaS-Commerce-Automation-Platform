Product Name
CommercePilot
Product Tagline
The AI Automation Layer for Existing E-commerce Businesses
Vision Statement
CommercePilot empowers small and medium-sized businesses by transforming existing e-commerce
platforms into AI-powered commerce systems.
Instead of replacing WooCommerce, Shopify, or custom online stores, CommercePilot acts as an
intelligent automation layer that automates operational work while keeping business owners in full
control.
The long-term vision is to become the operating system for AI-driven commerce automation.
Mission
Reduce repetitive operational work by at least 80% while improving order accuracy, response time, and
customer satisfaction through responsible Artificial Intelligence.
Product Philosophy
CommercePilot is NOT another e-commerce website.
1
CommercePilot is NOT a chatbot.
CommercePilot is an AI Employee that works alongside business owners.
Humans remain responsible for business decisions.
Artificial Intelligence performs repetitive operational work.
Why This Product Exists
Most SMEs already own an online store.
Their biggest challenge is running the business efficiently.
Every day they manually:
Read WhatsApp messages
Understand customer requests
Check products
Create orders
Reply to customers
Send quotations
Send invoices
Track orders
Update inventory
These repetitive tasks consume valuable business time.
CommercePilot automates these workflows.

Core Value Proposition
Instead of asking businesses to replace their current e-commerce platform,
CommercePilot upgrades their existing platform with AI automation.

Product Principles
AI Assists Humans

Existing Systems First
Never replace customer software.

Modular Design

Explainable AI

Multi-Tenant by Default
