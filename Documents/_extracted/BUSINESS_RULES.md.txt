1. Purpose
This document defines the official business rules for CommercePilot.
All business logic must follow these rules.
If implementation conflicts with this document, this document takes precedence.
2. Business Philosophy
CommercePilot automates repetitive operational work.
CommercePilot never replaces business decision-making.
Artificial Intelligence assists.
Humans decide.

Each tenant has isolated:
Users
Customers
Products
Orders
Messages

Settings
Cross-tenant access is strictly prohibited.
5. Customer Rules

Existing customer → attach conversation

9. Product Matching Rules
AI must only match products from the tenant's catalog.
Never invent products.
If multiple products match:
Return the best matches with confidence.
If no product matches:
Request clarification from the customer .
10. Confidence Score Rules
Confidence Score Range:
0.00 – 1.00
0.95–1.00 Automatic draft creation.
0.80–0.94 Owner review recommended.
Below 0.80 Manual confirmation required before order creation.
Confidence score must be stored with every AI decision.

14. Inventory Rules
Before approval:
Validate stock.
If stock is unavailable:
Flag the order .
Notify the owner .
Suggest alternatives (future feature).
Inventory is never reduced until the order is officially approved and synchronized.

21. Error Handling Rules
If AI fails:
Save the message.
Notify the owner .
Allow manual processing.
If WooCommerce fails:
Retry using queue.
Do not lose data.
Never silently ignore failures.

24. AI Learning Rules
CommercePilot does not automatically retrain AI using customer conversations.
Business data belongs to the tenant.
No tenant data may be shared with another tenant.
Future model improvements require explicit owner consent where applicable.

26. Definition of Business Correctness
A feature is business-correct only if:
✓ It follows all business rules.
✓ It respects tenant isolation.
✓ It supports owner approval.
✓ It never invents products.
✓ It maintains audit logs.
✓ It preserves customer history.
✓ It prevents duplicate orders.
✓ It integrates safely with WooCommerce.
✓ It supports future platform expansion.
Final Principle
CommercePilot exists to automate repetitive business operations while preserving human control over
critical business decisions.
Every workflow should reduce manual effort, improve accuracy, and maintain trust.
Whenever business efficiency and business safety conflict, business safety takes priority.
