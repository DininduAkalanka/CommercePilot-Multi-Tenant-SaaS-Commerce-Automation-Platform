2. Project Vision
CommercePilot is an AI-powered commerce automation platform designed to integrate with existing e-
commerce systems rather than replace them.
The platform connects to an existing online store (WooCommerce, Shopify, or custom APIs), monitors
customer communication channels such as WhatsApp, understands customer intent using Large
Language Models, automates repetitive business processes, and assists business owners with
intelligent order processing.
1
CommercePilot is not a chatbot.
CommercePilot is an AI Employee.
3. Mission
Reduce manual work performed by online business owners through AI-driven workflow automation
while maintaining full human control over critical business decisions.
4. Problem Statement
Many SMEs receive orders through WhatsApp instead of directly through their websites.
Business owners manually:
Read customer messages
Extract order details
Check inventory
Create orders
Send quotations
Reply to customers
Update inventory
Notify staff
This process is:
Slow
Error-prone
Difficult to scale
Expensive
CommercePilot automates these repetitive tasks while allowing the owner to review and approve AI
decisions when required.

6. Core Philosophy
The system must NEVER replace the business owner .
The system assists the owner .
Human approval always has higher authority than AI.
AI recommendations are suggestions.
The owner always has the final decision.
7. Product Principles
AI First
Every repetitive task should be evaluated for automation.
Human in the Loop
Critical business decisions require owner approval unless explicitly configured otherwise.
Existing Systems First
CommercePilot never replaces existing e-commerce systems.
CommercePilot extends them.
Multi-Tenant by Design
Every customer business is isolated.
No tenant data may ever be accessible by another tenant.
Tenant isolation is mandatory.•

13. AI Principles
The AI does not make business decisions.
The AI recommends.
The owner approves.
The AI must never hallucinate products.
The AI must use Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) against the tenant's product catalog before
making recommendations.
Confidence scores must determine whether human review is required.
